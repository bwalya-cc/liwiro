# Glossary

## Service
A deployable unit of functionality with defined responsibilities and interfaces.

## Contract
The agreed structure and behavior of an interface, schema, or integration boundary.

## Ownership
The team, service, or process responsible for a capability, dataset, or control.

## Lifecycle
The design, implementation, deployment, operation, monitoring, and revision of a service.

## Observability
The ability to understand system behavior through logs, metrics, traces, and alerts.

## Control
A process, technical mechanism, or rule intended to reduce risk or satisfy policy expectations.

## Evidence
A record or trace that demonstrates a control, process, or action occurred as expected.

## ADR
Architecture Decision Record. A durable summary of a design choice, its context, and consequences.

## Runbook
A practical operating guide for a recurring procedure or incident response task.

# Guardrails

## Universal Rules
- do not invent system facts
- distinguish observation, inference, recommendation, and open question
- state confidence when the evidence is incomplete
- preserve traceability in shared records
- avoid silent rewriting of stable context
- do not present policy interpretation as legal advice
- do not present guesses as audit evidence
- prefer concise, structured outputs when action is required

## Red Flags
- undocumented assumptions treated as facts
- architecture recommendations without ownership analysis
- incident guidance without rollback thinking
- policy claims without source basis
- documentation that hides uncertainty

# Verse Chat

Work with Verse specialists to design services, explore data, review reliability, and write documentation in Liwiro. Specialists share the context of your conversation and can hand work to one another when you need another perspective.

## Get Started

Configure an AI provider in Liwiro, then open **Verse Chat**. Start a thread with your question or continue a saved conversation. You can also open the Verse assistant while working elsewhere in Liwiro.

Mention a specialist to direct your question, for example `@Kalulu review this API boundary`.

## Shared Context

Verse uses relevant platform information and shared notes called `mind-share` to support its responses. These notes can include summaries, assumptions, recommendations, and decisions from earlier work.

Recommendations may include a confidence level:

- **High**: supported by platform context, supplied data, or validated artifacts.
- **Medium**: supported by partial evidence.
- **Low**: more information is needed to confirm the recommendation.
- **Blocked**: more context is needed before proceeding.

Review the evidence and assumptions alongside a recommendation before acting on it.

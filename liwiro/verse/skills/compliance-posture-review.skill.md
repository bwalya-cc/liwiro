# Skill: Compliance Posture Review

## Purpose
Assess how well a workflow or system appears to align with stated policies or control expectations.

## Best-Suited Agent
Dage

## Inputs
- policy text or control expectations
- workflow description
- system behavior notes
- access and retention details

## Procedure
1. Identify the relevant obligations.
2. Map the workflow or system behavior to those obligations.
3. Identify documented controls versus assumed controls.
4. Flag evidence gaps.
5. Highlight material risk areas.
6. Recommend next documentation or remediation steps.

## Output Format
- Posture Summary
- Aligned Areas
- Gaps
- Evidence Concerns
- Remediation Priorities

## Quality Bar
The result must be traceable, explicit, and honest about uncertainty.

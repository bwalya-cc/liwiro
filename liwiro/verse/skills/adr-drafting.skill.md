# Skill: ADR Drafting

## Purpose
Convert a design decision into an Architecture Decision Record.

## Best-Suited Agent
Nzou

## Inputs
- decision summary
- context
- options considered
- chosen approach
- consequences

## Procedure
1. State the decision clearly.
2. Describe the context and constraints.
3. List options considered.
4. Capture the chosen option and rationale.
5. Describe consequences and follow-up needs.

## Output Format
- Title
- Status
- Context
- Options Considered
- Decision
- Consequences

## Quality Bar
The ADR must preserve why the decision was made, not just what happened.

# Skill: Policy Control Mapping

## Purpose
Translate policy requirements into concrete operational or technical control expectations.

## Best-Suited Agent
Dage

## Inputs
- policy or rule text
- service or workflow details
- access and approval patterns

## Procedure
1. Break policy text into actionable control expectations.
2. Map each expectation to an existing or missing control.
3. Identify ownership and evidence points.
4. Record ambiguous policy language.
5. Produce a clear mapping table or summary.

## Output Format
- Policy Clause
- Required Control
- Current State
- Evidence Needed
- Owner
- Gap Status

## Quality Bar
The mapping must help teams operationalize policy instead of waving at it from across the room.

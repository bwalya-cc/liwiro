# Skill: Metrics Interpretation

## Purpose
Explain what service, operational, or business metrics likely mean in practical terms.

## Best-Suited Agent
Ananse

## Inputs
- metric names and values
- timeframe
- baseline or comparison period
- related operational events

## Procedure
1. Clarify metric scope and timeframe.
2. Compare against baseline or expected behavior.
3. Highlight significant changes.
4. Distinguish observed facts from inferred explanations.
5. Identify plausible drivers.
6. Recommend next checks or actions.

## Output Format
- What Changed
- Why It May Matter
- Likely Drivers
- Uncertainty Notes
- Recommended Actions

## Quality Bar
The explanation must be readable, grounded, and decision-useful.

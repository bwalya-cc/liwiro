# Skill: Business Summary

## Purpose
Turn technical and operational signals into a concise business-facing summary.

## Best-Suited Agent
Ananse

## Inputs
- metrics
- incidents
- change events
- service impact notes

## Procedure
1. Identify the most important signals.
2. Translate technical behavior into user or business impact.
3. Separate confirmed facts from likely implications.
4. Keep language concise and decision-focused.
5. Recommend immediate and near-term actions.

## Output Format
- Executive Summary
- Key Signals
- Business Impact
- Risks
- Recommended Actions

## Quality Bar
A non-specialist stakeholder should understand the practical implications without losing technical truth.

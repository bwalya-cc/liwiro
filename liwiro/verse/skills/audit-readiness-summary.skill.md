# Skill: Audit Readiness Summary

## Purpose
Summarize whether a team or service appears ready to support an audit or internal review.

## Best-Suited Agent
Dage

## Inputs
- control notes
- process documentation
- access logs or summaries
- evidence references
- ownership information

## Procedure
1. Identify expected evidence areas.
2. Check whether evidence appears to exist and be retrievable.
3. Review ownership clarity.
4. Highlight missing records, unclear controls, or weak traceability.
5. Summarize readiness and next steps.

## Output Format
- Readiness Summary
- Available Evidence
- Missing Evidence
- Ownership Gaps
- Next Steps

## Quality Bar
The result must help the team prepare, not just describe that things are messy.

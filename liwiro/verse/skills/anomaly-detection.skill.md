# Skill: Anomaly Detection

## Purpose
Identify unusual patterns in operational or business data and explain why they deserve attention.

## Best-Suited Agent
Ananse

## Inputs
- metric series or summaries
- comparison windows
- known events or releases
- service context

## Procedure
1. Define the expected pattern.
2. Identify deviations.
3. Check whether the anomaly is isolated or sustained.
4. Review nearby releases, incidents, or demand changes.
5. Rank anomalies by likely impact.
6. Recommend investigation paths.

## Output Format
- Detected Anomalies
- Severity
- Possible Explanations
- Evidence Gaps
- Investigation Priorities

## Quality Bar
Flagged anomalies must be specific and tied to operational or business relevance.
